Essay 1 · Formal Email Writing
Tulis email formal kepada klien Mr. Tanaka untuk meminta jadwal meeting minggu depan.
📌 Format wajib: Subject · Greeting · Body (tujuan + min. 2 pilihan waktu + minta konfirmasi) · Sign-off + nama.
Subject: Meeting Request — Week of October 20

Dear Mr. Tanaka,

I hope this email finds you well. I am writing to request a meeting to discuss our upcoming project collaboration.

Could we schedule a meeting on Tuesday, October 21, at 10 AM, or Thursday, October 23, at 2 PM? Please let me know which time is most convenient for you.

I look forward to your confirmation. Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
[Your Name] | [Your Position]
Essay 2 · Phone Conversation Dialogue
Buat dialog telepon (min. 8 baris) antara Receptionist dan Caller.
📌 Alex dari PT Maju Jaya menelepon Ms. Sari (Finance), tapi Ms. Sari sedang rapat. Alex meninggalkan pesan. Gunakan min. 3 dari: hold on / put through / leave a message / call back / speak up / breaking up.
R: Good morning, PT Sukses Abadi. This is Rini speaking. How may I help you?
C: Good morning. This is Alex from PT Maju Jaya. Could I speak to Ms. Sari from Finance, please?
R: Please hold on a moment. I will check if she is available.
C: Of course, thank you.
R: I'm sorry, Ms. Sari is in a meeting right now. Would you like to leave a message for her?
C: Yes, please. Tell her Alex called about the invoice payment. My number is 0812-3456-7890.
R: Certainly. She will call you back at 0812-3456-7890.
C: Perfect. Thank you very much.
R: You're welcome. Thank you for calling. Have a nice day!
Essay 3 · Grammar in Context (5 Kalimat)
Buat 1 kalimat untuk SETIAP poin (konteks perkantoran).
  1. Gunakan must not untuk aturan kantor
  2. Gunakan I am going to... untuk rencana pasti
  3. Gunakan Past Simple irregular verb "send" (V2: sent)
  4. Gunakan by untuk deadline
  5. Gunakan OSASCOMP order: noun phrase dengan 3+ adjectives
1. You must not use your personal phone during the client presentation.
2. I am going to present the Q3 report on Monday — it is already confirmed.
3. She sent the invoice to the client yesterday afternoon.
4. Please submit the monthly report by 5 PM today.
5. We need a beautiful large modern rectangular wooden meeting table for the new office. (O-S-A-Sh-M ✓)
Essay 4 · Short Recount Text
Tulis Recount Text singkat (min. 5 kalimat) tentang kegiatan kerja.
📌 Pilih: (a) Site visit ke pabrik · (b) Business trip · (c) Training
Struktur: Orientation (who/when/where) → Events (First/Then/Finally + Past Tense) → Re-orientation
[Orientation] Last Tuesday, I went to Bandung for a business trip with my supervisor, Mr. Budi, to visit our main supplier.

[Events] First, we arrived at the factory at 9 AM and met the production manager. Then, we reviewed the production line and identified several delays. After that, we attended a meeting where we proposed new solutions. Finally, we signed a revised agreement before heading back to Jakarta.

[Re-orientation] Overall, the trip was very productive, and I felt proud that we found solutions that will save the company time and money.
Essay 5 · Internal Memo / Short Note
Tulis memo internal singkat kepada rekan kerja bernama Rina.
📌 Kamu harus pergi meeting mendadak. Beritahu: (1) ke mana, (2) kapan kembali, (3) 1-2 tugas yang Rina bantu. Gunakan imperative sopan (Please/Kindly + V1).
To: Rina · From: Deni · Date: October 13, 2026

Hi Rina,

I am in an emergency meeting with the director and will be back at around 3 PM. Please print the monthly sales report and put it on my desk. Kindly also reply to Mr. Hasan's email — he is asking about next week's delivery schedule. The details are in my inbox.

Thank you so much!
Deni
