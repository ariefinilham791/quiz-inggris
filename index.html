🔵 Modal Verbs — Fungsi & Contoh

can / could
Kemampuan · I can speak English / Masa lalu: I could swim as a child
will
Janji/keputusan spontan · I will help you! (baru diputuskan)
would
Polite request / kebiasaan lalu · Would you like coffee?
may / might
Kemungkinan · may (lebih yakin) vs might (kurang yakin)
shall
Usul bersama / tawaran · Shall we start? / Shall I help?
must
Kewajiban internal (dari diri sendiri) · I must submit today
have to
Kewajiban eksternal (aturan orang lain) · I have to wear a helmet
should / ought to
Saran · You should rest / You ought to see a doctor
must not
⛔ DILARANG keras · You must not smoke here
don't have to
✅ Tidak perlu (bukan larangan) · You don't have to come

🟡 Future: Will vs Going To

will
✦ Keputusan SPONTAN saat berbicara
✦ Janji (promise)
✦ Prediksi umum
"The phone rings → I will answer it!"
going to
✦ Rencana SUDAH diputuskan sebelumnya
✦ Ada bukti fisik yang jelas
"I already bought tickets → I'm going to fly"
(-) Negatif
will not (won't) / am-is-are not going to
By vs Until
by = batas akhir/deadline · Submit by Friday
until = durasi · Wait until 5 PM

✍️ Latihan Essay

5 soal seperti ujian · Tulis jawabanmu lalu cek panduan

Essay 1 · Formal Email Writing
Tulis email formal kepada klien bernama Mr. Tanaka untuk meminta jadwal meeting minggu depan.
📌 Kamu adalah staff admin. Gunakan format email lengkap: Subject, Greeting, Body (tujuan + min. 2 pilihan waktu + minta konfirmasi), Sign-off + nama.
💡 Lihat Panduan & Contoh ▼
Contoh Jawaban:

Subject: Meeting Request — Week of October 20

Dear Mr. Tanaka,

I hope this email finds you well. I am writing to request a meeting to discuss our upcoming project collaboration.

Could we schedule a meeting on Tuesday, October 21, at 10 AM, or Thursday, October 23, at 2 PM? Please let me know which time is most convenient for you.

I look forward to your confirmation. Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
[Your Name] | [Your Position]
Essay 2 · Phone Conversation Dialogue
Buat dialog telepon (min. 8 baris) antara Receptionist dan Caller.
📌 Situasi: Alex dari PT Maju Jaya menelepon untuk Ms. Sari (Finance Dept), tapi Ms. Sari sedang rapat. Alex meninggalkan pesan. Gunakan min. 3 phrasal verb: hold on / put through / leave a message / call back / speak up / breaking up.
💡 Lihat Panduan & Contoh ▼
R: Good morning, PT Sukses Abadi. This is Rini speaking. How may I help you?
C: Good morning. This is Alex from PT Maju Jaya. Could I speak to Ms. Sari from Finance, please?
R: Please hold on a moment. I will check if she is available.
C: Of course, thank you.
R: I'm sorry, Ms. Sari is in a meeting right now. Would you like to leave a message for her?
C: Yes, please. Could you tell her that Alex called about the invoice payment? My number is 0812-3456-7890.
R: Certainly. I will let her know. She will call you back at 0812-3456-7890.
C: Perfect. Thank you very much.
R: You're welcome. Thank you for calling. Have a nice day!
Essay 3 · Grammar in Context (5 Kalimat)
Buat 1 kalimat untuk SETIAP poin (total 5 kalimat), dalam konteks perkantoran.
  1. Gunakan must not untuk aturan kantor
  2. Gunakan I am going to... untuk rencana pasti
  3. Gunakan Past Simple irregular verb "send" (V2: sent)
  4. Gunakan by untuk deadline
  5. Gunakan reflexive pronoun (himself/herself/themselves)
💡 Lihat Panduan & Contoh ▼
1. You must not use your personal phone during the client presentation.
2. I am going to present the Q3 report on Monday — it is already in my calendar.
3. She sent the invoice to the client yesterday afternoon.
4. Please submit the monthly report by 5 PM today.
5. The CEO reviewed the entire proposal himself because no one else was available.
Essay 4 · Short Recount Text
Tulis Recount Text singkat (min. 5 kalimat) tentang kegiatan kerja.
📌 Pilih salah satu: (a) Site visit ke pabrik · (b) Business trip ke kota lain · (c) Training yang kamu ikuti

Struktur wajib: Orientation (who/when/where) → Events (First/Then/Finally + Past Tense) → Re-orientation (kesimpulan)
💡 Lihat Panduan & Contoh ▼
[Orientation] Last Tuesday, I went to Bandung for a business trip with my supervisor, Mr. Budi, to visit our main supplier.

[Events] First, we arrived at the factory at 9 AM and met the production manager. Then, we reviewed the production line and identified several delays in the delivery schedule. After that, we attended a joint meeting where we proposed new solutions. Finally, we signed a revised agreement before heading back to Jakarta.

[Re-orientation] Overall, the trip was very productive, and I felt proud that we found solutions that will save the company both time and money.
Essay 5 · Internal Memo / Short Note
Tulis memo internal singkat kepada rekan kerja bernama Rina.
📌 Kamu harus pergi meeting mendadak. Beritahukan: (1) kamu ke mana, (2) kapan kembali, (3) ada 1-2 tugas yang perlu Rina bantu.
Gunakan kalimat imperative yang sopan (Please/Kindly + V1).
💡 Lihat Panduan & Contoh ▼
To: Rina
From: Deni
Date: October 13, 2026

Hi Rina,

I am in an emergency meeting with the director and will be back at around 3 PM. Please print the monthly sales report and put it on my desk. Kindly also reply to Mr. Hasan's email — he is asking about next week's delivery schedule. The details are in my inbox.

Thank you so much!
Deni
